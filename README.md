Vice Coast Cover Studio (round 9 — the cast cover) is an offline web app that turns your photos and a caption into multi-panel GTA-style cover art: a cast of five original painted characters with slanted name tags, six painted coastal scenes (dawn beach, sunset bay, neon night street with a full-profile car, an aerial coast, a blue-hour skyline, a golden coast road), comic slanted layouts, six colour grades, and full poster dressing — press quote with stars, starburst seal, palm-strip footer, billing block, barcode and monogram. Crop, pan, zoom and flip any photo per panel, fill every panel with one photo or spread many across the cover, tune the poster finish per panel, and export PNGs at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 and 1920 × 1080. Everything is original art and lettering drawn by the app itself; photos are read on your own machine and never uploaded.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
# hand the entry in with: mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
